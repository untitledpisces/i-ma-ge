In Short

Singgah Sana is a private estate, not a hotel. The entire property is yours during your stay, and we ask only that you treat the house, the land, and the village with quiet care. Below is the full picture.

These policies apply to every booking made at Singgah Sana, whether through our website, by direct enquiry, or through a partner. If anything here is unclear, write to us — we would rather clarify than have you guess.

I

Booking & Payment

All bookings at Singgah Sana are confirmed by a written agreement between us and the lead guest. The lead guest is responsible for the full booking, including the conduct of every person in their party.

  • 1.1

    Deposit. A 50% deposit is required to confirm your booking. The deposit is applied to the total cost of your stay and is non-refundable except as set out in Section IV.

  • 1.2

    Balance. The remaining balance is due 30 days before arrival. For bookings made within 30 days of arrival, the full amount is payable at the time of booking.

  • 1.3

    Security deposit. A refundable security deposit may be requested for event bookings or larger groups. This is returned within 7 days of departure, less any costs for damage or additional cleaning.

  • 1.4

    Methods. We accept bank transfer, credit card, and selected e-wallets. All rates are quoted in Malaysian Ringgit (MYR) and inclusive of applicable taxes.

II

Arrival & Departure

The estate is a home, prepared for you before you arrive. To give our team enough time to ready every pavilion, we keep to the following times.

  • 2.1

    Check-in. From 3:00 PM onwards. Earlier arrival may be possible on request, subject to availability.

  • 2.2

    Check-out. By 12:00 PM. Late departure may be arranged in advance and may incur an additional fee.

  • 2.3

    Directions. Precise directions and a location pin are shared with your booking confirmation. We recommend arriving before dark, particularly for first-time visitors to Janda Baik.

III

During Your Stay

The estate is yours to enjoy quietly. To keep it a place worth returning to, we ask that you observe a few simple expectations.

  • 3.1

    Quiet hours. Between 11:00 PM and 7:00 AM, we ask that noise be kept low out of respect for the village and the forest. Amplified music is not permitted outdoors at any time.

  • 3.2

    Smoking. Smoking is not permitted inside any pavilion. Designated outdoor areas are available.

  • 3.3

    Alcohol. Alcohol is welcome and may be brought with you or arranged through us. Please drink responsibly.

  • 3.4

    Fire. Open fires are permitted only in the designated fire pit and only when attended. Guests are not permitted to light fires elsewhere on the estate.

  • 3.5

    The river. Swimming in the river is at your own risk. Children must be supervised at all times near the water.

  • 3.6

    Wildlife. The estate is surrounded by forest. Please do not feed the wildlife and take care with your belongings in outdoor areas.

Note The estate is a working home with staff present during certain hours. We will always let you know in advance when someone will be on the grounds.
IV

Cancellations

We understand that plans change. Our cancellation policy reflects the small scale of the estate — one booking, at most, at a time.

  • 4.1

    More than 30 days before arrival. Full refund of any amount paid, less a small administrative fee.

  • 4.2

    Within 30 days of arrival. The deposit is non-refundable but may be transferred to another date within 12 months, subject to availability.

  • 4.3

    Within 7 days of arrival. No refund and no transfer. We encourage travel insurance for all bookings.

  • 4.4

    By us. In the rare event that we must cancel your booking, you will receive a full refund of all amounts paid, or the option to move to alternative dates.

V

Children & Pets

The estate is a beautiful place for children and, on a case-by-case basis, for well-behaved pets.

  • 5.1

    Children. Warmly welcome. Cots, high chairs, and child-friendly meals can be arranged. Please let us know ages when you book so we can prepare the right pavilion.

  • 5.2

    Supervision. Children remain the responsibility of their parents or guardians at all times, particularly near the river, the fire pit, and the forest edge.

  • 5.3

    Pets. Well-behaved pets are welcome with prior arrangement and a small additional cleaning fee. Pets are not permitted on beds or upholstered furniture.

VI

Events & Gatherings

Events at Singgah Sana are held under a separate agreement, in addition to this policy. The following applies to all gatherings.

  • 6.1

    Exclusivity. Every event booking includes exclusive use of the entire estate. There are never two events on the same day.

  • 6.2

    Vendors. We work with a trusted circle of local vendors, but you are welcome to bring your own, subject to a brief site induction. We do not charge vendor commissions either way.

  • 6.3

    Curfew. Amplified music must end by 11:00 PM. All guests other than overnight guests must depart by midnight.

  • 6.4

    Weather. All event layouts include a covered alternative. Events proceed in rain or shine.

VII

Privacy & Data

We collect only the information we need to host you well. We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for anything other than your stay.

  • 7.1

    What we collect. Name, contact details, booking dates, guest count, and any preferences you share with us.

  • 7.2

    How we use it. To prepare for your stay, to communicate with you, and to keep records required by law. We do not market to you without consent.

  • 7.3

    Retention. Booking records are kept for 7 years to meet Malaysian tax and hospitality requirements. Any additional information is deleted after your stay.

  • 7.4

    Photography. We never photograph guests or their belongings without permission. Any images taken at the estate for promotional purposes are always arranged in advance.

VIII

Liability

We take every reasonable care to keep the estate safe. The following outlines the limits of our responsibility.

  • 8.1

    Personal belongings. Singgah Sana is not responsible for loss or damage to personal belongings. Safes are provided in each pavilion.

  • 8.2

    Activities. Guests participate in estate activities (river swimming, forest walks, trekking) at their own risk. We recommend appropriate footwear and caution.

  • 8.3

    Force majeure. We are not liable for events beyond our control, including extreme weather, road closures, or government restrictions.

  • 8.4

    Damage. Guests are responsible for any damage to the estate or its contents caused during their stay. Repair or replacement costs will be charged at cost.

Recommendation We strongly recommend comprehensive travel insurance for all guests, covering cancellation, medical, and personal belongings.
IX

Contact & Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time. The version in effect at the time of your booking is the version that applies to your stay. Significant changes will be communicated by email to guests with active bookings.

For any questions about this policy, or to request a copy in a different format, please reach us at the details below.
