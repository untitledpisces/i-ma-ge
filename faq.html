I

Stay & Rooms

Up to 16 guests can stay overnight across the four pavilions. For day events, the estate can host up to 80 standing guests or 40 seated at dinner. Final capacity depends on layout and is confirmed after your site visit.
The pavilions are designed for natural ventilation with ceiling fans and cross-breezes. Janda Baik sits at elevation, so evenings and mornings are naturally cool. Air-conditioning is not part of the estate's design philosophy — we find our guests rarely miss it.
Yes. Every booking — whether a single pavilion or the entire estate — is entirely private. There are no shared spaces with other guests, no strangers on the grounds, and never two bookings at once.
For stays of two guests or a small family, a single pavilion can be booked on quieter weeks. The estate grounds remain yours to use. For events or larger groups, the entire estate is required.
II

Rates & Booking

Rates vary by pavilion, season, and length of stay. A single pavilion begins from RM 1,200 per night; the entire estate from RM 4,800 per night. Full rates are shared on enquiry, along with any seasonal minimums.
A 50% deposit confirms your booking. Cancellations more than 30 days before arrival are refunded in full, less a small administrative fee. Within 30 days, the deposit is transferable to another date within 12 months. Full policy is shared with your booking confirmation.
For weddings and large gatherings, 6–12 months in advance is ideal. For smaller stays, 6–10 weeks is usually sufficient. Peak months — June to August, and December — book earliest. We always recommend enquiring before you set your dates.
III

Getting Here

Singgah Sana sits in Kampung Janda Baik, Bentong, Pahang — about 45 minutes from central Kuala Lumpur and just over an hour from KLIA. The last stretch is a quiet village road; precise directions and a pin will be shared with your booking confirmation.
Yes — we can arrange private transfers from KLIA, Subang, or anywhere in Kuala Lumpur through our trusted drivers. Rates are shared on request. Many guests also drive themselves; there is ample covered parking at the estate.
Standard check-in is 3:00 PM and check-out is 12:00 PM. Early arrival and late departure are possible subject to availability — just ask when you book. For event bookings, the estate is yours from dawn.
IV

Experience & Dining

Breakfast is included with every stay. Lunch, dinner, and the Golden Hour Table are available on request, prepared by our estate cook using produce from the garden and the local market. Vegetarian, vegan, and halal menus are all available.
Morning walks, river swims, and access to the estate grounds are included. Curated experiences — artisan sessions, guided treks, cooking classes, and the Golden Hour Table — can be arranged before or during your stay.
Yes, warmly. The estate is a beautiful place for children — river, lawn, orchard, and open space. We can arrange cots, high chairs, and child-friendly meals. Please let us know ages when you book so we can prepare.
We welcome well-behaved pets on a case-by-case basis, with prior arrangement. There is a small additional cleaning fee. Please mention your pet when you enquire so we can prepare the pavilion accordingly.
Yes — reliable Wi-Fi is available across the estate, including in each pavilion. That said, many guests choose to leave their phones in the room. The river does not have a signal.
V

Policy & Practicalities

The pavilions are strictly non-smoking; smoking is permitted only in designated outdoor areas. Alcohol is welcome and can be arranged through us, or brought with you. We ask only that noise is kept considerate after 11:00 PM, out of respect for the village.
The estate is built on gently sloping ground with traditional raised pavilions. Some pathways are gravel and a few steps are unavoidable. We can arrange ground-level access for guests with limited mobility — please tell us in advance so we can prepare the right pavilion and route.
Comfortable walking shoes, a light rain jacket, swimwear for the river, and a good book. Everything else — organic linens, robes, incense, mosquito nets, and toiletries — is provided in your pavilion. The estate is a place for less, not more.
